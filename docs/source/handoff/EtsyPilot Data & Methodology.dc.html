Methodology
How every number in EtsyPilot is produced, what it excludes, and when it should not be trusted. Last reviewed Aug 12, 2026.

The five classes
VerifiedEtsy returned it for your own shop. Exact.
CalculatedA visible formula over visible inputs. Reproducible.
EstimatedModelled from observable signals. Always a range.
Seller inputYou or a teammate entered it. We never guess it.
UnavailableEtsy does not expose it. We show nothing, not a guess.
Keyword demand Estimated
SourcePublic autocomplete and listing-count signals, sampled weekly.
MethodSignals are normalised into a monthly band. We publish the band, never a midpoint.
FreshnessUpdated weekly · last Aug 10, 2026
ConfidenceModerate for common terms, low below 100 listings.
CoverageUS, UK, CA, AU · 4 locales
Limitations. Etsy publishes no search volume. This is not Etsy search data and does not represent Etsy's internal figures. Treat two terms with overlapping bands as equal.
Net profit Calculated
Gross revenue − Etsy fees − payment processing − Offsite Ads − shipping − COGS − labour − other costs = net profit
VerifiedRevenue, Etsy fees, payment processing and Offsite Ads come from your receipts.
Your inputShipping, COGS, labour and other costs come from your cost setup.
Coverage62% of order value has a confirmed cost
ScenariosIn the base scenario, fee lines are Verified — Etsy reported them. When a scenario varies volume, the fees that follow are relabelled Calculated: Etsy never confirmed a hypothetical.
Exclusions. Orders with no confirmed product cost are excluded rather than assigned an assumed cost, so net profit is a floor. Sales tax, VAT and refunds outside the period are not included.
Shop Pulse baseline Calculated
MethodA rolling 90-day expected range from your own order history, by weekday. Unexplained dips are measured on the residual — net of what the recorded changes already explain — so the same shortfall is never counted twice.
DiagnosisRecorded events inside the window are tested against the outcome and labelled CORRELATED, RULED OUT or UNKNOWN.
Coverage88% · 11 listings too new to baseline
Limitations. Correlation is not cause. Ninety days cannot separate seasonality from a change you made. Etsy does not release views or impressions, so diagnoses rest on orders alone.
What we never do
Model or claim knowledge of Etsy's ranking algorithm. Publish an exact competitor revenue or sales figure. Fill a missing number with an assumption to make a chart look complete. Scrape Etsy pages, automate Etsy Messages, or hold your Etsy password. Build a profile of an individual buyer, or show a region with too few orders.
