Phase 1 · Foundations
Design direction: make smarter Etsy decisions with data you can trust
1 — Provenance is the product. Every number carries a Verified / Calculated / Estimated / Seller input / AI draft / Unavailable badge.
2 — Estimates are ranges with confidence, never falsely precise integers.
3 — Calm light canvas; borders and spacing do the structural work, not shadows or gradients.
4 — One obvious primary action per screen; secondary actions stay quiet.
5 — Blue is EtsyPilot. Etsy appears only as a labeled connected platform, never as brand colour.
6 — Shop context and sync freshness are permanently visible in the top bar.
7 — Unavailable data explains why and offers a supported next action instead of a number.
8 — Bulk and destructive work is a stepper: validate → preview → confirm → progress → rollback.
9 — AI output is always a draft with source chips and an explicit human approval step.
10 — Mobile is re-composed around actions and summaries, not a shrunken dashboard.
