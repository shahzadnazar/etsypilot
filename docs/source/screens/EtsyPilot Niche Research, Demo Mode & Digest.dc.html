What would have to be true EtsyPilot will not tell you to enter a niche. These are the conditions the numbers imply.
1You can sell above $34 — below the band's midpoint, 41,000 listings compete on price alone. 2Your product cost stays under $14 to clear 40% margin after fees at that price. 3You are listing by early October — demand roughly doubles Oct to Dec and the prep window closes before it. 4You differentiate on something other than the word "linen" — the top 10 shops hold 31% and all use it.
