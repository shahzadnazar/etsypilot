EP EtsyPilot Step 3 of 5 · Connect your shop Save & exit
Etsy
Connect your Etsy shop You’ll sign in on Etsy and approve access. EtsyPilot never receives your Etsy password.
Choose what EtsyPilot can access
Read shop & listings — required Lets us show your listings, tags, prices and inventory, and run audits. Scopes: listings_r, shops_r
Manage listings Needed to publish edits, bulk changes and scheduled updates. Without it, EtsyPilot can only suggest changes. Scope: listings_w
Orders & financials Powers profit, fees, sales map and delivery status. Buyer locations are aggregated — we never build buyer profiles. Scopes: transactions_r, billing_r
Inventory updates — optional Only if you want EtsyPilot to adjust quantities from automation rules.
EtsyPilot never receives your Etsy password. Access is granted by Etsy and can be revoked at any time from Settings → Data permissions or from your Etsy account.
