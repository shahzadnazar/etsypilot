PRD gap closure · Shop Pulse & Profit Reality
The two screens the PRD needs and the design did not have
Shop Pulse (PRD 4.2) monitors the shop against its own historical baseline. It never claims access to Etsy's ranking algorithm — it correlates observable events with observable outcomes, and says plainly when it cannot.
Profit Reality (PRD 4.7) replaces the old Profit & fees waterfall with the full eight-cost line, editable inputs and three scenarios — one screen, scenarios as a tab, not a second surface.
Both reuse the existing shell, cards, tables, inputs and provenance badges unchanged. The only new visual vocabulary is the diagnosis badge family below, deliberately square so a diagnosis can never be mistaken for a data source.
The sidebar in both screens uses the PRD §9 information architecture — Dashboard / Research / Listings / Profit / Tools / Data — as a concrete answer to the three-nav-tree question.
03b · Diagnosis badge family — extends Foundations 03
Provenance answers where a number came from. Diagnosis answers whether an event explains a change. They must never be confused, so diagnosis badges are 6px-radius rectangles with a leading rule, not 999px pills, and each carries an icon and a word — never colour alone.
CORRELATED
An observable change and an observable outcome moved together inside the comparison window.
Always paired with the evidence that produced it. Correlation is stated as correlation, never cause.
RULED OUT
The change was tested against the outcome and does not account for it.
Kept visible rather than hidden — knowing what did not cause a drop is half the diagnosis.
UNKNOWN
A change was observed but the available evidence cannot explain the outcome.
The honest default. Etsy's ranking behaviour is never offered as an explanation.
Every diagnosis carries a comparison window, the events tested, and a confidence level. A diagnosis is never shown without the evidence panel that produced it — the badge is an entry point, not a verdict.

Implementation notes
Both screens reuse the existing shell, KPI card, table, tabs, input, coverage-warning and provenance-badge components without modification. The diagnosis badge is the one addition — three variants, square, 6px radius, leading rule. Profit Reality replaces the old Profit & fees waterfall rather than sitting beside it: Discounts and Refunds move into the transactions tab, since the PRD's cost line does not include them. Scenarios and Costs are tabs on this screen, so the PRD's Profit group resolves to one surface with three views.
Every figure here is sample data continuous with the other screens — Willow & Fern, 438 orders, $18,420.65 gross. Net profit is $4,937.15 — computed from the eight cost lines, not stated independently — rather than the old $5,138.20 because labour, payment processing and other costs are now counted.
