## Notes for the lawyer reviewing this

Points that need specialist attention:

1. **Jurisdiction.** The entity's country is not yet decided. Most customers will be UK, EU and US
   consumers. The governing-law and consumer-rights clauses (§9, §15) need to be checked against
   where the entity is actually established and where it sells.
2. **Merchant of record.** If Paddle or Lemon Squeezy is used, the contractual chain changes — they
   become the seller of record to the customer. §9 and §13 need to reflect the actual arrangement.
3. **Etsy's API Terms, Section 4** requires executed Application Terms with each Etsy seller,
   covering what is collected, how it is used, stored, secured and disclosed, and the controls the
   seller has. These terms are intended to satisfy that, and must be accepted by each seller at
   connection, not merely linked in a footer.
4. **Etsy's API Terms, Section 3** requires a warranty disclaimer naming the developer as sole
   provider. §12 above carries it; confirm the exact wording against the current Etsy requirement.
5. **The liability cap in §13** should be checked against what is enforceable in the chosen
   jurisdiction for consumer contracts.
6. **§6, the no-training commitment.** It is a promise about conduct rather than a description of
   the service, and it is the one Etsy's API Terms key on in Section 5 — confirm that a promise in
   this form binds us and binds our processors, and that the notice route in §14 is an adequate way
   to change it.
